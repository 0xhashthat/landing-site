Intro

Independent security reviews for software teams

HashThat LLC is a software security consultancy. We provide code audits, security reviews and secure development support to engineering teams, with a focus on smart contracts and blockchain-based systems.

We also help teams harden the desktop and mobile environments they work in. Whatever the engagement, the approach is the same: understand the system, find the risks that matter, and deliver clear, practical recommendations to address them.

Work

What we do

Cybersecurity Consulting

Threat modeling, vulnerability assessments and secure architecture design, focused on smart contracts and blockchain-based systems.

Smart Contract Auditing

In-depth manual code review backed by test-driven analysis. We identify logic flaws and economic design weaknesses, and back every finding with a reproducible test.

Incident Analysis

Technical root-cause analysis after a security incident, with remediation guidance to fix the affected code and prevent it from happening again.

Security Research & Writing

Articles and research on secure coding practices and threat awareness.

About

Who we are

HashThat brings more than eight years of hands-on experience in cybersecurity and blockchain research and development. We specialize in helping teams that build on blockchain ship safer, more reliable software.

Whether it's auditing a protocol before launch, tracing the root cause of an incident, or writing well-tested smart contracts, we bring the same security-first mindset, careful methodology and clear communication to every engagement.

We work with clients on a project or retainer basis under written service agreements.
